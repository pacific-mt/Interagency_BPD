BPD & FL Normal Range Checker

INTERGROWTH-21st International Fetal Growth Standards
Interpretation in this tool uses the INTERGROWTH-21st 10th–90th centile range. For gestational ages between completed weeks, reference limits are linearly interpolated between the adjacent exact-week values.

This tool is for screening/triage support and does not provide a diagnosis. Measurement technique and clinical assessment remain important.
